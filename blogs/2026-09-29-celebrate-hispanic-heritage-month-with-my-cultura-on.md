Recent research from iHeartMedia and Collage Group, " New American Consumer: Bicultural Latinos ," spotlights a powerful and growing force in American culture: Bicultural Latinos. Identifying as equally American and Hispanic, this group now represents nearly 40% of the U.S. Latino population, and the connection to their heritage is only deepening.
